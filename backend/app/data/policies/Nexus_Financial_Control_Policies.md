# Nexus Logistics Global — Fleet & Freight Financial Control Policies

**Document ID:** POL-NEX-2026-01  
**Classification:** Logistics Operations & Supply Chain Control  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  

---

## 1. Fleet Maintenance & Port Towing Authorization
- Fleet diesel refueling and vehicle maintenance invoices over **$5,000.00** require authorization from the VP of Transportation Operations.
- Weekend wire disbursements exceeding **$2,500.00** for emergency port crane towing or container repairs require written sign-off from the Fleet Operations Manager.

---

## 2. Duplicate Fuel & Freight Invoice Controls
- Accounts payable must verify intermodal shipping invoice numbers against port gate logs before releasing funds.
- Duplicate freight charges matching identical vendor IDs, dollar amounts, and invoice references processed within 10 days must be flagged for payment recovery.

---

## 3. Customs Broker & Third-Party Vendor Compliance
- Disbursements to customs clearance brokers or port drayage operators exceeding **$1,500.00** require verified tax IDs and active port security clearances. Unverified vendors must be placed on immediate payment pause.

---

## 4. Telematics & Logistics Software Subscription Surges
- Telematics and fleet tracking subscriptions with monthly cost increases exceeding **15%** must undergo vehicle seat auditing before renewing annual agreements.
