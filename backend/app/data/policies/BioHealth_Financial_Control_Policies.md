# BioHealth Dynamics Inc. — Enterprise Financial Control & Compliance Policies

**Document ID:** POL-BIO-2026-01  
**Classification:** Internal Financial Controls & Clinical Compliance  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  

---

## 1. Clinical Trial & Lab Procurement Authorization
- All specialized biological reagents, cell culture media, and laboratory equipment purchases exceeding **$5,000.00** must be backed by a verified Purchase Order (PO) signed by the Chief Scientific Officer (CSO).
- Off-hours weekend wire disbursements exceeding **$2,000.00** for clinical trial patient recruitment or emergency lab supplies are strictly prohibited without dual sign-off from the Finance Director.

---

## 2. Duplicate Invoice & Equipment Verification Controls
- Accounting personnel must cross-reference invoice reference numbers against historical clinical trial disbursements prior to approving payment releases.
- Duplicate invoices matching identical vendor names, amounts, and reference numbers within a 14-day window must be placed on immediate payment hold.

---

## 3. Vendor Compliance & FDA W-9 Verification
- No disbursement exceeding **$1,000.00** shall be made to biological chemical suppliers or clinical trial recruiters flagged as "Unverified" or lacking active W-9 tax documentation and FDA compliance registration.

---

## 4. Software & EDC Subscription Price Surge Policy
- SaaS platforms supporting Electronic Data Capture (EDC) or clinical data management experiencing price surges exceeding **15%** year-over-year must be referred to the IT Procurement Committee for tier auditing.
